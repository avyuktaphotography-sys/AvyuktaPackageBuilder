GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

Everything you need to know before your session.

How early should I enquire about my photoshoot?

We recommend enquiring 2–4 weeks in advance to help find a suitable date.

What photoshoot time slots are available?

Our standard session timings are 07:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 01:00 PM, 04:00 PM and 07:00 PM. Each slot is subject to confirmation.

Where is your photography studio located?

Our studio is located near Electronic City in Bengaluru.

Do you offer home and outdoor photoshoots?

Yes. We offer both indoor studio sessions and photography at your preferred location.

Are family photographs included?

Yes. Our baby photography packages include 2–3 family photographs.

Can I add a reel or cinematic video?

Yes. An Instagram reel is available for an additional ₹3,000, and a 3–4 minute cinematic video is available for an additional ₹15,000.

Does submitting an enquiry confirm my slot?

No. Submitting an availability enquiry does not reserve or confirm a photoshoot. Our team will review your preferred date and time and respond to you directly.
