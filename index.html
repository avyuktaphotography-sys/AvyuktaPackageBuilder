We recommend enquiring 2–4 weeks in advance.

Our standard timings are 07:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 01:00 PM, 04:00 PM and 07:00 PM. Each slot is subject to confirmation.

Our studio is located near Electronic City in Bengaluru.

Yes. We offer both studio and location-based photography sessions.

Yes. Instagram reels are available for an additional ₹3,000 and cinematic videos for an additional ₹15,000.
